1. Introduction to Blockchain
Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that stores transaction records in blocks. These blocks are connected to each other using cryptography.
- It is decentralized, meaning no single authority controls it.
- Transactions are recorded digitally.
- Once recorded, data is very difficult to change.
- Every transaction is verified by network participants.
- It provides security, transparency and trust.
2. Features of Blockchain
1. Decentralization: No single person or organization controls the entire blockchain network.

2. Peer-to-Peer Network: Users can directly transfer information or cryptocurrency without an intermediary.

3. Immutability: Once data is added to the blockchain, it is very difficult to modify or delete.

4. Tamper-Proof: Blockchain uses cryptographic hashes to detect unauthorized changes in data.
3. Cryptocurrency
Cryptocurrency is a digital currency that uses cryptography for secure transactions.
- It works on blockchain technology.
- It can be transferred electronically.
- It does not require a traditional bank for peer-to-peer transfers.
- Examples: Bitcoin, Ether and Litecoin.
4. Transaction Wallets
A cryptocurrency wallet is a digital wallet used to store and manage crypto assets and interact with blockchain networks.

Types of keys:
- Public Key/Address: Used to receive cryptocurrency.
- Private Key: Used to authorize transactions. It must be kept secret.
Types of wallets:
- Hot Wallet: Connected to the internet.
- Cold Wallet: Stored offline.
5. Ether Transaction
Ether (ETH) is the native cryptocurrency of the Ethereum blockchain.

An Ether transaction is the process of transferring ETH from one wallet address to another.

Steps involved:
1. Open the MetaMask wallet.
2. Click on Send.
3. Enter the receiver's wallet address.
4. Enter the amount of ETH.
5. Check the gas fee.
6. Confirm the transaction.
7. The transaction is broadcast to the Ethereum network.
8. After verification, it is recorded on the blockchain.
Gas Fee: Gas fee is the transaction fee paid to process transactions on the Ethereum network. It is generally paid in ETH.
6. MetaMask
MetaMask is a cryptocurrency wallet that allows users to store, send and receive Ether and interact with Ethereum-based applications.

Features:
- Creates and manages crypto wallets.
- Sends and receives ETH.
- Connects with decentralized applications (DApps).
- Allows users to view transaction history.
- Supports Ethereum and compatible networks.
7. Benefits of Blockchain
1. Time-saving: Transactions can be processed without traditional intermediaries.
2. Cost-saving: Reduces the need for third-party services.
3. Security: Uses cryptography to protect transaction records.
4. Transparency: Transactions can be verified on the blockchain.
5. Decentralization: No central authority controls the network.
2. Installation procedure of MetaMask








samsclass.info


1. Open Google Chrome browser.
2. Visit the official website: https://metamask.io.
3. Click on Download and select Chrome.
4. Click Add to Chrome.
5. Click Add Extension.
6. Open the MetaMask extension.
7. Select Create a New Wallet.
8. Create a strong password.
9. Read and accept the terms and conditions.
10. Reveal and safely write down the 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.
11. Verify the recovery phrase in the correct order.
12. Click Confirm.
13. Your MetaMask wallet is successfully created.
Important
 Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private key with anyone.
3. Procedure to receive and send Ether








remote3.co


To receive Ether:
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Select your Ethereum account.
3. Copy your public wallet address.
4. Share it with the sender.
5. Wait for the transaction confirmation.
To send Ether:
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Click Send.
3. Enter the receiver's wallet address.
4. Enter the amount of ETH.
5. Check the estimated gas fee.
6. Click Next.
7. Review the transaction details.
8. Click Confirm to send.
4. Spending Ether per transaction
Ether transaction cost consists of:
- Transfer Amount: ETH sent to the receiver.
- Gas Fee: Fee paid for processing the transaction.
Formula:
\[
\text{Gas Fee}=\text{Gas Used}\times\text{Gas Price}
\]

\[
\text{Total ETH Spent}=\text{Transfer Amount}+\text{Gas Fee}
\]

Example:
Particulars	Amount
ETH transferred	0.01 ETH
Gas fee (example)	0.001 ETH
Total spent	0.011 ETH

These are illustrative values, not actual network fees.
